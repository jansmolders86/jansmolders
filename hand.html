HAND THEREMIN

🖐️ Play with your hands

Your right hand sets the pitch (higher hand = higher note). Your left hand sets the volume. Your fingers shape the sound.

Note
–
Volume (left hand height)
–
Brightness (open / close hand)
–
Vibrato (pinch thumb + index)
–
Fingers up → harmony
–
Pitch hand (right):
↕ height → note
✊→🖐️ open hand → brighter tone
🤏 pinch thumb + index → vibrato
☝️✌️🤟 fingers up → adds harmony (1 = solo, 2 = +fifth, 3 = major chord, 4 = +octave, 5 = full chord)

Volume hand (left): higher = louder. No left hand? Volume stays at medium.

Take your pitch hand out of view to go silent. Good light helps tracking.
