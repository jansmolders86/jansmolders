HAND THEREMIN

🖐️ Play with your hands

Your right hand sets the pitch: higher hand, higher note. Your left hand sets the volume. Your fingers shape the sound.

📱 Prop your phone up about an arm's length away so both hands are free. Turn the silent switch off on iPhone.
Pitch hand (right side of the screen):
↕ height → note
✊→🖐️ open your hand → brighter tone
🤏 pinch thumb + index → vibrato
☝️✌️🤟 fingers up → harmony (1 solo · 2 fifth · 3 major chord · 4 + octave · 5 full chord)

Volume hand (left side): higher = louder. With only one hand, volume stays at medium.

Move your pitch hand out of view to go silent. Good, even light helps the tracking.
