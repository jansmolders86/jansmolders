About

A designer who never left the workshop.

I have been a designer for over 15 years.

I've worked at Epic Games for more than a decade as well as for ad agencies across the world. Born and raised in the Netherlands, I have worked on Branding, UI/UX, Game Assets, Concept Art, Level design, Marketing and Web-Design & Development for various games and properties.

Today that runs through one studio: game development on one side, brand and marketing on the other, and the craft that connects them in the middle.
